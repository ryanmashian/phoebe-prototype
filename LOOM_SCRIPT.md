## Voice notes

- **Don't say** "AI", "automation", "smart", "intelligent", "powered by", "platform", "predictive analytics."
- **Do say** "Sentinel," "Scheduler," "agent," "queue ranker," "pre-warm," "signal."
- **Pacing**: terse, declarative. Coordinator voice, not pitch voice.
- **Don't sell**. The product is the demo. Let it carry.
- **Smile only on the last line**. Everything before is ops-floor.

## Two micro-cuts that punch up the open

If you want a colder open, drop the first 8 seconds and start at the dashboard with no audio, let the banner read for 3 seconds, then cut to:

> "This is the predictive layer for the Phoebe Scheduler. Same agents. Twelve hours earlier."

That version is 50 seconds and ends harder.
